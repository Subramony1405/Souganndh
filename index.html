About us

A little story behind SringarabySouganndh.

SringarabySouganndh is a jewellery journey built with love, creativity and a deep appreciation for the beauty of Indian adornment. What began as a passion for beautiful pieces and meaningful styling grew into a little space where tradition, colour and individuality come together.

We believe jewellery is more than an accessory — it becomes part of your celebrations, your memories and the way you express yourself. Every collection we bring is chosen with that feeling in mind, with a promise to serve our customers with warmth, care and gratitude.

After an unexpected personal commitment took us away from the business for a while, we are beginning again — with a renewed page, fresh energy and the same love for jewellery. Thank you for finding us again and for being part of our journey.

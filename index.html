About us

A little story behind SringarabySouganndh.

SringarabySouganndh is a jewellery journey built with love, creativity and a deep appreciation for the beauty of Indian adornment. What began as a passion for beautiful pieces and meaningful styling grew into a little space where tradition, colour and individuality come together.

We believe jewellery is more than an accessory — it becomes part of your celebrations, your memories and the way you express yourself. Every piece we share is selected with that feeling in mind, with warmth, care and gratitude.

After taking a pause from the page for a personal commitment, we are beginning again with fresh energy and the same love for jewellery. Thank you for finding us again and for being part of our journey.
